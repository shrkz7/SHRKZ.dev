# Alex Carter Freelancer Portfolio (Canadian Market Edition)

A high-performance, conversion-engineered portfolio website tailored specifically for an Indian student/freelance developer targeting Canadian local businesses and marketing agencies.

---

## 🌟 Key Features Built-In

1. **Canadian Localization & Trust Elements:**
   - Mentions local Canadian business hubs (Toronto, Vancouver, Calgary, Montreal, Halifax).
   - Invoicing transparency in **Canadian Dollars (CAD)** with 0% forex markup badges.
   - Built-in **CASL (Canada Anti-Spam Legislation)** compliance note in the footer.
   - 50/50 milestone payment protection structure.

2. **The "Overnight Advantage" Live Clocks Hub:**
   - Real-time JavaScript clocks for **Toronto (EST)**, **Calgary (MST)**, **Vancouver (PST)**, and **India (IST)**.
   - Automatically detects Canadian business hours vs. your overnight development sprint.

3. **High-Converting "Spec" Case Studies:**
   - **Calgary Roofing & Exteriors:** Speed remediation from 7.2s to 1.1s + 1-click quote UX.
   - **Vancouver MedSpa:** Boutique aesthetic redesign with appointment booking integration.
   - **Toronto Digital Agency Sprint:** White-label overflow under strict NDA.
   - Interactive category filter tabs (All, Local Businesses, Agency White-Label).

4. **Interactive CAD Project & Milestone Estimator:**
   - Instant calculation for Starter Redesigns (\$750 CAD), Full Local Business Sites (\$1,450 CAD), and Agency Sprints (\$1,200 CAD).
   - Dynamic calculation of optional add-ons (Booking APIs, Local SEO, Monthly Retainers).
   - Automatically calculates 50% deposit and 50% milestone sign-off amounts.

5. **Free 5-Minute Video Audit Lead Capture:**
   - Interactive form requesting Canadian province/city and website URL with realistic verification simulation.

## 🛠️ How to Customize for Yourself

1. **Replace the Name & Branding:**
   - Open [`index.html`](file:///C:/Users/sharukesan/.gemini/antigravity/scratch/freelancer-portfolio/index.html) and search for `Alex Carter` or `AlexCarter.dev`. Replace with your actual name or studio name (e.g., `Rohit Sharma` / `SummitDev.com`).
2. **Update Contact Links:**
   - Replace `alex@carterwebstudio.com` with your professional custom domain email.
   - Replace the LinkedIn placeholder with your verified LinkedIn profile URL.
3. **Connect a Real Form Service (Free):**
   - In [`index.html`](file:///C:/Users/sharukesan/.gemini/antigravity/scratch/freelancer-portfolio/index.html), replace the `<form id="auditForm">` with a free Formspree or Web3Forms action URL (e.g. `action="https://api.web3forms.com/submit"`), or connect it to your email in [`script.js`](file:///C:/Users/sharukesan/.gemini/antigravity/scratch/freelancer-portfolio/script.js).
4. **Embed Your Calendly / Cal.com:**
   - Change the `Book Discovery Call` button href from `#contact` to your direct [Cal.com](https://cal.com) or [Calendly](https://calendly.com) link.

## 🌐 3-Minute Free Deployment (Vercel / Netlify)

1. Create a free account at [Vercel](https://vercel.com) or [Netlify](https://netlify.com).
2. Drag and drop this `freelancer-portfolio` folder directly into the Netlify or Vercel dashboard.
3. Your site will immediately be live on a fast global CDN with free SSL certificate (`yourname.vercel.app`).
4. Go to **Settings > Domains** and connect your custom domain (`yourname.com`).
